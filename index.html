“Can it close the awning when the sun hits my desk?” Yes. “Can the shades open at sunrise, even though it’s a different time each day?” Yes. “Can I add a new rule after handover?” Yes — tell the local agent. These are examples, not a fixed menu.

Sunrise

05 — Retrofit

If it has a remote, it can join the system.

Most homes already have good hardware — it’s just not connected. We use IR, RF, relays, bridges and sensors to bring existing devices into the same system. No rip-and-replace. No second app. No new wall clutter.

Questions

The things designers ask.

If yours isn’t here, send a message — we answer within one working day.

Do you work white-label?

Yes. We can produce drawings, schedules and client-facing documentation under your studio’s name, and stay invisible on site if that’s how you prefer to work. Or we can be introduced as your automation partner. Either way, the client relationship stays yours.

We already have an electrical or AV contractor. Can you work with them?

Yes — that’s usually the best setup. We define the automation scope and the interfaces, then coordinate with your contractor on power, network, ceiling and joinery requirements. We’re happy to work to their programme.

What do you need from us to quote?

A floor plan, a brief on how the client lives, any device list they already have, and a few site photos if available. If drawings aren’t ready, a rough plan and a 20-minute call is enough to start.

Can you automate devices the client already owns?

That’s our default. ACs, awnings, projectors, curtains, lamps and older audio can often be brought in through IR, RF, relays and bridges. We audit what’s there first and only recommend replacement when the existing device genuinely can’t be integrated.

Do you sell hardware?

We’re vendor-neutral. We can supply and commission, or specify for your vendor to supply. Hardware is billed at actuals with your approval, and we don’t design around a single brand’s catalogue.

How early should we involve you?

Concept or design development is ideal — that’s when power, network, ceiling and joinery decisions are still open. We can also retrofit at the end of a project; it just means a few more visible compromises.

How do you charge?

A fixed project fee based on scope, not hourly billing. The blueprint is a standalone deliverable; if we take the project through commissioning, that fee is credited. Studio partners get trade rates and priority slots. See engagement models.

What about privacy and data?

Camera, voice and automation data stay on a hub inside the home by default. There is no mandatory cloud subscription and no vendor access to the client’s footage unless they explicitly grant it for support. The full picture is in our privacy note.

Do you work outside Mumbai?

We’re based in Mumbai and work across the city, with MMR projects on request. Projects outside the region are possible for the right scope — travel and site supervision are quoted separately.

Can we visit a live project?

For serious enquiries, yes — subject to the client’s permission. We can also demonstrate the system and the app over a video call, which is usually faster.
